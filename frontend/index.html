How it works

One phone. One family. Five steps.

Built for the phone a household already shares: voice first, an icon on every choice, and every answer read aloud.

Ask

The family picks a language. A parent gives consent, read aloud, before anything is stored.

Understand

Kinvia hears who is speaking and picks up each concern — income, status, safety, distance, cost.

Show evidence

Earnings and placement for that trade, in that district, with source, date and sample size.

Plan the path

Course to job to NSQF level-up to diploma, plus the schemes the learner may qualify for.

Share & follow up

A Family Trust Card for the relatives, and a live counsellor for whatever is still open.

Features

The model explains. Verified data supplies every number.

Family Trust Card

Evidence the whole family can hold.

Decisions get made later, with grandparents and relatives who were never in the room. Every session ends with a card in the family’s own language — the trades discussed, the sourced figures, each concern and how it was answered, and the next step. Share it on WhatsApp, save it, or print it.

Grounded answers

No invented salaries.

The model never writes a figure. A grounding check blocks any earnings or placement number that isn’t backed by a verified record — and says so plainly instead of guessing.

If there is no record for your district, Kinvia tells you, and offers a counsellor.

Real concerns, not FAQs

It answers what parents actually ask.

Will he earn enough? Log kya kahenge? Is it safe for my daughter? Can she study further? Each question is recognised, attributed to whoever asked it, and answered with local evidence.

Income · Social status · Safety · Further study · Cost · Job security · Distance

Built for a shared phone

Voice first, for low literacy.

Speak instead of typing, hear every answer read back, tap an icon instead of reading a list, and switch between speakers mid-conversation — all on one handset.

Hindi and English today; the architecture takes 22 languages.

A person, one tap away

Machine triage, human care.

Kinvia answers the routine doubts. When a family wants a person, the counsellor receives the summary and the open concerns already attached — so nobody has to repeat their story.

Privacy

Built for families — and for minors.

Most learners here are under 18, so consent is the first screen, not a checkbox buried in settings.

Consent first

An accessible, read-aloud consent step before any personal information is stored.

A parent agrees

A parent or guardian consents before a minor’s information is kept at all.

Only what’s needed

District, schooling, interests and income bracket — never an exact income or address.

Who sees what

Counsellors see only the families who asked for them; administrators see aggregates.

Not kept forever

Raw audio is discarded after transcription; transcripts expire.

Always a way out

Ask for a person, withdraw consent or request deletion at any point.
